A local photo sieve for Android

Cribrum finds images on your device that you can delete or recompress: duplicates, blurry shots, messaging clutter, and large camera photos. All analysis runs on the device, suggestions require your confirmation, and deleted files stay in a safe trash.

The images in the screenshots are drawn in code, and the counts are made up.

Clean: what it finds

Cribrum scans storage, including folders hidden from your gallery, across six distinct phases with live progress and time estimates. Scans monitor device temperature and throttle if the phone gets warm. Photos marked as gallery favourites display a heart badge, start kept, and trashing a favourite asks for confirmation. Pages apply at once, with an immediate undo snackbar.

Exact copies
Matches identical files byte-for-byte using xxHash, marking the best copy to keep.
Similar copies
Finds recompressed and resized copies with perceptual hashes and MobileCLIP embeddings, suggesting the highest-quality version to keep.
Bursts
Clusters photos taken in quick succession and suggests keeping the sharpest shot with open eyes.
Blurry
Measures sharpness with Laplacian variance on subject areas, preserving intentional portrait blur.
Too dark or too bright
Identifies underexposed pocket shots, black frames, and blown-out frames using luminance histograms.
Screenshots
Detects screen captures by path, screen-matching dimensions, and absent camera metadata.
Documents and notes
Detects slides, whiteboards, handwritten notes, receipts, and printed pages using text coverage and MobileCLIP classification.
Forwarded
Flags memes and greeting images in messaging folders lacking camera metadata using zero-shot classification.
WhatsApp stickers
Finds reaction stickers saved in WhatsApp sticker folders across storage.

Photo compression

Instead of deleting photos, Cribrum can recompress large camera JPEGs to reclaim storage space.

Three quality levels
Choose High quality at JPEG 90 and original resolution, Balanced at JPEG 85 and up to 50 MP, or Smallest files at JPEG 80 and up to 12 MP. Balanced halves file size with little visible change.
Split comparison slider
Compare up to three photos before confirming. A draggable split handle separates the original and the compressed copy, with deep zooming down to individual pixels.
Metadata and gain maps
Copies keep EXIF tags, GPS coordinates, date taken, camera details, and Ultra HDR gain maps on Android 14 and newer. Cribrum requests system confirmation to retain gallery favourite status on compressed copies.
Reversible originals
Original photos move to Cribrum's trash folder. Undo the entire batch with one tap from the summary screen, or restore individual originals from the trash at any time.
Background batches
Compresses photos in the background with progress notifications. Batches can be stopped at any time and pause when the phone gets hot.

Settings and storage map

Settings gives you control over trash retention, folder exclusions, and scanning sensitivity.

Storage map
An interactive treemap shows where storage goes across your folders. Select a folder to inspect its size and file count, or exclude it from scans.
Trash retention
Deleted files and original copies stay in Cribrum's trash for 30 days by default, adjustable from 7 to 90 days in settings. Restore photos to their original folders or empty the trash permanently.
Detection thresholds
Fine-tune recall with sensitivity sliders for each category, or reset them to defaults.
System dark theme
The app follows Android's system dark mode setting automatically, adjusting colors and contrast for low light.

Design principles

Practical over pretty
A clean, minimal UI whose features actually work.
Recall over precision
Surfacing an image that turns out to be worth keeping is fine. Missing clutter that could have been deleted is not.
Private by design
All scanning and analysis run on your device. No photos, hashes, or metadata ever leave your phone. Cribrum explicitly removes network permissions.
Nothing is lost by accident
Every deletion and compression is confirmed and reversible. Files move to Cribrum's trash folder on shared storage, kept safely before permanent deletion, and can be restored to their original paths.

Permissions and sideloading

Standard gallery applications use Android MediaStore, which only indexes public media folders. MediaStore ignores folders with .nomedia markers, such as WhatsApp sent media folders, where silent storage waste accumulates.

Cribrum asks for All files access, known as MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, to scan hidden directories directly and manage its trash folder on shared storage. Because Google Play restricts this permission to file managers, Cribrum is distributed as a direct APK download on GitHub.

Cribrum explicitly removes the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its manifest, stripping telemetry from bundled libraries. The app cannot open network connections or send data anywhere.

Install

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Get the latest release from the GitHub releases page. You can optionally verify package integrity with the SHA-256 digest:

    sha256sum -c cribrum-*.apk.sha256
  2. 2

    Allow unknown apps

    Open the downloaded package. When Android asks to allow installation from your browser or file manager, confirm to proceed.

  3. 3

    Grant storage access

    On first launch, grant All files access when Android prompts in system settings, so Cribrum can explore hidden folders across storage.

  4. 4

    Scan and review

    Tap Scan now to run the initial scan, or leave it to run automatically overnight while charging. Review clutter candidates grouped by category, or recompress large photos from the Compress tab.
